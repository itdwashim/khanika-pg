Page options

Background theme

Changes the colours of the whole website and the student portal.

Rooms, rent and bed availability come from the desktop app (Settings > Website & Online Portal > Update website).

Flyer photo

The big picture at the top of the website, shown in a 1200 × 900 frame (landscape, 4:3). Any photo is cropped from the centre to fit. If there is no flyer, nothing is shown there.

Photo gallery

Photos appear in the gallery on the website, in this order. If you add no photos, the original gallery is shown.

Student login by mobile OTP

Students can log in to the portal with an OTP sent by SMS to their registered mobile number. The SMS is sent by an SMS service that you pay for: create an account with one of them and paste its details here. In India, SMS services usually ask you to register a sender name and message template (DLT); their help pages guide you.

The page sends {"mobile","otp","message"} as JSON to this address (with the key as a Bearer token if you entered one).
